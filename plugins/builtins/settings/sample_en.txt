No matter what I gain now,
No matter what I hold now,
Again and again and again—
Still can’t reach it out.

Say I’m aiming way too high now,
Rotting kind of words now,
Everyone, everyone—
Wants to stand out.

There’s only one reason now,
I just wanna break down now,
This empty soul now.

Back when I could see it clearly,
Everything was shining brightly,
Even what I tried to hold on
Fades and bleeds out.

I don’t wanna die now,
I just wanna live loud,
Want it now, want it now—
Hear my soul shout.

This irrational feeling,
I can’t stop it burning,
Can’t forgive, can’t forgive—
Who I am now.

Pathetic, so pathetic,
Falling short in everything now,
Just a surface kind of crying
Hides it somehow.

“Just obey and stay quiet,”
Dirty kind of guidance,
Even shutting out the noise
Feels like I bow.

All alone I’m breaking,
Nowhere left for aching,
Kicked into the hollow,
Echoing out.

So empty I can’t even cry now,
Smoldering but there’s no sound now,
Even things I wanted to do
Blur and fade out.

Even what I tried to reach now
Breaks and melts down.

I don’t wanna vanish,
I just want it shattered.

Rusting, twisting noises,
Warped and broken voices,
Clouded “clarity” inside—
Shatter it out.

Pathetic, so pathetic,
I can’t take it now.

Meaning’s cut and fallen,
Breathing slowly stalling,
Dreams are just illusions
Worn and burned out.

Won’t resist, won’t fight now,
Only left with fear now.
